### 1.4 Vegetation Types

| Type | Coverage | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Cerrado (savanna)** | 59.22% | Dominant biome, representing 94.84% of the APA |
| **Forest-savanna contact zones** | 27.89% | Transition between cerrado and mata atlantica |
| **Refugial vegetation** | 12.89% | Including campos rupestres |
| **Mata Atlantica fragments** | 5.16% | On eastern slopes (humid side) |

Specific vegetation formations across the region:
- **Campos rupestres**: Herbaceous-shrubby communities on quartzite/sandstone substrates, found above 900-1000m elevation. Extremely high endemism.
- **Cerrado rupestre**: Rocky grassland-savanna transitions on slopes
- **Capoes de mata montana**: Forest patches ("capoes") on steep slopes and depressions
- **Mata ciliar (gallery forests)**: Along waterways
- **Mata Atlantica fragments**: Humid forests on the eastern escarpment

### 1.5 Access Without ICMBio Permit vs. With Permit

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION - APA vs. PARNA:**

An APA (Area de Protecao Ambiental) is a **sustainable use** category under Brazil's SNUC (Sistema Nacional de Unidades de Conservacao). Unlike a PARNA (full protection), an APA:
- Allows private land ownership within its boundaries
- Permits regulated economic activities (agriculture, tourism, etc.)
- Does NOT require a general entry permit for visitors
- Has zoning that defines allowed activities per zone

The Parque Nacional (PARNA) da Serra do Cipo, which sits INSIDE the APA, is **full protection** (protecao integral):
- Requires entry through official portarias (gates) during operating hours (8:00-17:00, entry until 14:00)
- Drone flights are **strictly prohibited** without ICMBio/SISBIO research authorization
- Commercial/tourist drone use is generally denied

**For drone orthomapping without SISBIO permit, the following areas WITHIN the APA but OUTSIDE the PARNA are potentially accessible:**

1. **Private lands along MG-010** (with landowner permission) - numerous fazendas and pousadas
2. **Cardeal Mota / district of Serra do Cipo village area** - lower elevation, mixed cerrado
3. **Areas between Santana do Riacho and Lapinha da Serra** - campos rupestres on private land
4. **Congonhas do Norte sector** - Serra Talhada area, remote campos rupestres
5. **Morro da Pedreira itself** (the climbing area) - outside PARNA boundaries

**However, even within the APA, drone operations must comply with:**
- ANAC regulations (RBAC-E No. 94)
- DECEA/SARPAS airspace authorization
- APA zoning restrictions (consult the 2014 Management Plan)
- Municipal regulations
- Landowner permission on private property

**Recommendation:** Contact the NGI (apa.morrodapedreira@icmbio.gov.br) to confirm whether specific drone flights in the APA buffer zone require SISBIO authorization or if APA zoning permits them with only ANAC/DECEA compliance.

## 2. Specific Sites Along MG-010 Road

### 2.1 MG-010 Overview

MG-010 is a paved highway running from Lagoa Santa (accessed via BR-040 from BH) northward through Jaboticatubas and into the Serra do Cipo. It is excellent pavement for roughly 100 km, after which conditions vary. Total distance from Belo Horizonte: ~100 km to the Serra do Cipo district.

### 2.2 Named Locations

#### Cardeal Mota / Serra do Cipo District (Km ~92-100)
- **Coordinates:** ~19 21'S, 43 36'W
- **Elevation:** ~750-850m
- **Terrain:** Lower elevation cerrado, transition to campos rupestres
- **Drone suitability:** GOOD - relatively flat terrain near village, open areas along river valleys, private properties with open campos. The village itself offers accommodations and infrastructure.
- **Notable:** Serra Morena waterfall is on private property outside the PARNA, near the district center. Access is commercial (paid entry).
- **Vegetation:** Mixed cerrado, gallery forests along Rio Cipo, some cultivated areas

#### Travessao (Vale do Travessao)
- **Access:** MG-010 Km 116 (or via Portaria Alto Palacio / Pousada Duas Pontes at Km 144)
- **Coordinates:** ~19 15'S, 43 32'W (upper valley area)
- **Elevation:** 1,200-1,400m
- **Trail:** 9 km from Portaria Alto Palacio (moderate), or 21 km round trip from Km 116 access
- **Terrain:** Monumental valley dividing Sao Francisco and Doce river basins
- **Drone suitability:** EXCELLENT for campos rupestres mapping. Wide open terrain, quartzite outcrops, minimal tree canopy. Natural amphitheater topography.
- **Vegetation:** Pure campos rupestres, rocky grassland, scattered Vellozia
- **CAUTION:** Inside PARNA - requires SISBIO authorization for drone

#### Canyon das Bandeirinhas
- **Access:** Via Portaria Areias, 10 km trail (flat, bikeable)
- **Coordinates:** ~19 23'S, 43 33'W
- **Elevation:** ~900-1,100m
- **Terrain:** Canyon with quartzite walls, nearby Braunas Waterfall (~65m, largest in PARNA)
- **Drone suitability:** EXCELLENT for geological/geomorphological mapping. Canyon walls provide dramatic quartzite exposure. However, canyon winds can be unpredictable.
- **Vegetation:** Campos rupestres on plateau above canyon, gallery forest in canyon floor
- **CAUTION:** Inside PARNA - requires SISBIO authorization

#### Cachoeira da Farofa
- **Access:** Via Portaria Areias, ~7 km trail (flat, well-marked)
- **Coordinates:** ~19 23'S, 43 34'W
- **Elevation:** ~850m
- **Terrain:** 25m waterfall with large pool, open cerrado/campos surrounding area
- **Drone suitability:** GOOD - open terrain approaching the waterfall, though the waterfall itself is in a valley
- **Vegetation:** Cerrado transition to campos rupestres
- **CAUTION:** Inside PARNA - requires SISBIO authorization

#### Capao Area
- **Coordinates:** ~19 17'S, 43 33'W
- **Elevation:** 1,200-1,400m
- **Terrain:** Isolated forest patches ("capoes") surrounded by campos rupestres. The capoes are forest islands in a matrix of open grassland on quartzite - a classic landscape ecology study system.
- **Drone suitability:** EXCELLENT for vegetation mosaic mapping. The contrast between dark forest patches and light rocky grassland makes for ideal orthomosaic classification.
- **Vegetation:** Capoes de mata montana, campos rupestres, cerrado rupestre

#### Lapinha da Serra
- **Access:** From Santana do Riacho, ~15 km dirt road
- **Coordinates:** ~19 07'S, 43 40'W
- **Elevation:** Village at ~1,100m; Pico da Lapinha at 1,647m; Pico do Breu at 1,687m
- **Terrain:** At the foot of one of the highest portions of the Serra do Cipo (Serra do Breu). Spectacular quartzite landscapes with exposed rock, caves, archaeological sites.
- **Drone suitability:** EXCELLENT - some of the most dramatic campos rupestres terrain. The approaches to Pico da Lapinha offer extensive open rocky terrain ideal for drone launch and mapping. Starting point for the famous Tabuleiro crossing (26 km).
- **Vegetation:** Campos rupestres with quartzite outcrops, gallery forests in valleys
- **Advantage:** OUTSIDE the PARNA boundaries (in the APA), so potentially accessible without SISBIO (but confirm with ICMBio)

#### Congonhas do Norte / Serra Talhada
- **Access:** From Conceicao do Mato Dentro, at the 160 km mark from BH, turn left at the junction toward Tabuleiro/Congonhas
- **Coordinates:** ~19 02'S, 43 28'W (Serra Talhada sector)
- **Elevation:** 900-1,600m
- **Terrain:** Northeast sector of Serra do Cipo. Remote, less visited, pristine campos rupestres. Serra Talhada has been the subject of floristic research since 1998.
- **Drone suitability:** EXCELLENT - very remote and undisturbed campos rupestres with minimal infrastructure. Open quartzite plateaus. Difficult access means fewer people/obstructions.
- **Vegetation:** Campos rupestres, campos de altitude, cerrado
- **Advantage:** Mostly OUTSIDE the PARNA; much of this area is within the APA or even outside it. Lower regulatory burden for drone flights.

#### Santana do Riacho Area
- **Access:** Along MG-010 between Km 92 and the Rio Cipo bridge
- **Coordinates:** ~19 19'S, 43 36'W (town center)
- **Elevation:** ~700-900m
- **Terrain:** Town is the administrative center for the PARNA. Surrounding area has cerrado, some campos rupestres at higher elevations, and agricultural areas.
- **Drone suitability:** MODERATE - lower elevation means less campos rupestres, but agricultural/cerrado mosaic mapping is possible. Good logistic base.

### 2.3 Best Campos Rupestres Exposures for Drone Mapping (Ranked)

1. **Travessao / Alto Palacio** (Km 121+) - highest elevation, purest campos rupestres, but inside PARNA
2. **Lapinha da Serra** - spectacular quartzite, partially outside PARNA
3. **Congonhas do Norte / Serra Talhada** - remote, pristine, mostly outside PARNA
4. **Canyon das Bandeirinhas area** - dramatic geology, but inside PARNA
5. **Capao area** - ideal forest-grassland mosaic, partially inside PARNA

### 2.4 Best Quartzite Outcrops

The entire Serra do Cipo is composed of Precambrian quartzite and sandstone (~1.7 billion years old). The most dramatic exposures are at:
- Lapinha da Serra (Pico da Lapinha / Serra do Breu)
- Travessao valley walls
- Canyon das Bandeirinhas
- Alto Palacio plateau (Km 121 MG-010)
- Areas above 1,200m along the main ridge

## 3. SISBIO Process for Drone Research

### 3.1 What is SISBIO?

SISBIO (Sistema de Autorizacao e Informacao em Biodiversidade) is ICMBio's online system for researchers to request authorization for:
- Collection of biological material
- Conducting research in federal conservation units (UCs)
- Research in caves
- Wildlife handling outside protected areas

**URL:** https://sicae.sisicmbio.icmbio.gov.br/usuario-externo/login
**Support email:** atendimento.sisbio@icmbio.gov.br

### 3.2 Who Can Apply?

- Researchers affiliated with recognized research institutions (universities, research centers)
- Higher education instructors
- The principal investigator must have a Lattes curriculum registered with CNPq

### 3.3 Required Registration Information

To register in SISBIO, you must provide:
1. Full name, CPF (Brazilian tax ID), correspondence address, and email
2. Identification of the scientific institution to which you are affiliated
3. Updated Lattes Platform curriculum (CNPq)

### 3.4 Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Register in SISBIO**
- Create account at https://sicae.sisicmbio.icmbio.gov.br/usuario-externo/login
- Provide personal, institutional, and Lattes data

**Step 2: Prepare and Submit Research Project**
- Title, objectives, methods, timeline
- Specify the UC(s) where research will take place (PARNA Serra do Cipo and/or APA Morro da Pedreira)
- List all researchers and collaborators
- Describe equipment to be used (HERE is where you detail drone/VANT specifications)

**Step 3: Specify Drone/VANT Details in the Project**
While SISBIO does not have a separate "drone permit" category, you MUST declare:
- Drone model, weight, camera specifications
- Flight plans (areas, altitudes, frequency)
- Purpose (vegetation mapping, geomorphological survey, etc.)
- Data handling and storage plan
- Proof of ANAC registration (if >250g) or exemption (if <250g)
- DECEA/SARPAS compliance intent

**Step 4: ICMBio Review**
- Processing time: **31 to 60 business days**
- ICMBio may request additional information
- The UC head (chefe da UC) reviews and may add conditions

**Step 5: Receive Authorization**
- Authorization document must be carried during all fieldwork
- Validity corresponds to the project timeline
- Must notify the UC management team before each field expedition

**Step 6: Maintain Compliance**
- Keep all project information updated in SISBIO
- Report any changes to researchers or methods
- Submit progress/final reports as required

### 3.5 Relevant Normative Instructions

- **IN ICMBio No. 3/2014** - Main regulation governing SISBIO authorizations (with DOU 18/06/2015 rectification)
- **IN ICMBio No. 7/2023** - Updated provisions (may include drone-specific guidance; full text should be consulted)
- **ICA 100-40 (DECEA)** - Drone airspace regulations (complementary to SISBIO)

### 3.6 Additional Authorizations Needed Beyond SISBIO

For drone research at Serra do Cipo, you need TRIPLE authorization:

| Authority | System | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **ICMBio** | SISBIO | Research authorization for the UC |
| **ANAC** | SISANT | Drone registration (if >250g); pilot credentials |
| **DECEA** | SARPAS NG | Per-flight airspace authorization |
| **ANATEL** | (homologation) | Radio frequency homologation of the drone |

### 3.7 Timeline Recommendations

- Start SISBIO application **at least 3-4 months** before planned fieldwork
- SARPAS authorization can be obtained closer to the flight date (days to weeks)
- ANAC/ANATEL registrations should be completed before SISBIO submission
- Plan for possible ICMBio requests for additional information (adds weeks)

### 3.8 Cost

SISBIO authorization is **free of charge**.

## 6. Seasonal Considerations for Drone Mapping

### 6.1 Climate Overview (Koppen: Cwb)

Serra do Cipo has a mesothermic climate with mild summers and a pronounced dry season.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| **Annual average temperature** | ~18 C |
| **Summer average** | 18-20 C |
| **Winter average** | Below 15 C |
| **Annual precipitation** | ~1,600 mm |
| **Elevation range** | 750-1,687m |

### 6.2 Seasonal Calendar

| Season | Months | Conditions | Drone Mapping Suitability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wet season (verao)** | October - March | Heavy rainfall, thunderstorms, high humidity, fog | POOR - frequent rain, clouds, poor visibility at altitude |
| **Dry season (inverno/seca)** | May - September | Low rainfall, humidity drops to 15%, clear skies, mild temperatures | EXCELLENT - best flying conditions, clear skies, stable air |
| **Transition (outono)** | April | Decreasing rain, still some cloud | GOOD - improving conditions |
| **Transition (primavera)** | October | Rains beginning, storms possible | MODERATE - unpredictable |

### 6.3 Fire Season

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Peak fire risk** | July - October (end of dry season) |
| **Worst months** | August - September |
| **Cause** | Both natural (lightning) and anthropogenic |
| **Humidity** | Can drop to 15% relative humidity |
| **Impact on drone mapping** | Smoke can reduce visibility and affect image quality. Post-fire landscapes provide valuable change-detection data. Active fires are dangerous for operations. |
| **Fire frequency** | Annual or periodic in campos rupestres (fire-adapted ecosystem) |

### 6.4 Flowering / Phenological Seasons

| Period | Phenological Event | Mapping Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **Year-round** | Some species always in flower ("floral carpets") | Campos rupestres have continuous but variable flowering |
| **November - February** | Peak flowering for many campo rupestre species | Best for biodiversity/phenological mapping |
| **May - September** | Reduced flowering, dry grasses, exposed soil/rock | Best for geological/geomorphological mapping; maximum quartzite exposure |
| **September - October** | Post-fire resprouting in burned areas | Excellent for fire recovery studies |

### 6.5 Recommended Windows for Drone Orthomapping

#### BEST OVERALL: May to August
- **Pros:** Dry, clear skies, stable air, low wind, no thunderstorms, maximum sun hours, low humidity (good for electronics)
- **Cons:** Vegetation drier/less green, fire risk toward August, cooler temperatures at high elevation

#### BEST FOR VEGETATION MAPPING: April to May
- **Pros:** End of wet season, vegetation still green, decreasing cloud cover, moderate temperatures
- **Cons:** Occasional rain still possible

#### BEST FOR GEOLOGICAL MAPPING: July to September
- **Pros:** Maximum quartzite exposure, dry grasses reveal rock structure, clearest skies
- **Cons:** Fire smoke risk (especially August-September), very dry air

#### BEST FOR PHENOLOGICAL/BIODIVERSITY MAPPING: November to February
- **Pros:** Peak flowering, maximum vegetation contrast between types
- **Cons:** Frequent rain interruptions, afternoon thunderstorms, reduced effective flying hours per day

### 6.6 Daily Flight Window

| Time | Conditions | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **06:00-08:00** | Morning fog/mist at high elevations | Wait for dissipation |
| **08:00-11:00** | IDEAL - calm air, good light, minimal shadows | **Priority flight window** |
| **11:00-14:00** | Strong light, minimal shadows (good for ortho) but thermals beginning | **Good for orthomosaics** (high sun = fewer shadows) |
| **14:00-16:00** | Thermals, possible convective clouds in wet season | Acceptable in dry season; avoid in wet season |
| **16:00-18:00** | Long shadows, cooling, wind decrease | Avoid (shadows compromise orthomosaic quality) |

### 6.7 Wind Considerations

- General wind: stronger at higher elevations (above 1,200m)
- Dry season: generally calmer winds
- Canyon areas (Bandeirinhas): unpredictable wind funneling
- Ridge tops (Travessao, Alto Palacio): exposed to stronger winds
- DJI Mini 3 maximum wind resistance: Level 5 (29-38 km/h); recommended operation below 20 km/h for mapping quality

## Summary: Recommended Strategy for Drone Orthomapping at Serra do Cipo

### Outside PARNA (No SISBIO Required - Verify with ICMBio)

1. **Lapinha da Serra** - Spectacular campos rupestres, quartzite outcrops, 1,100-1,687m. Outside PARNA boundary. Contact local landowners.
2. **Congonhas do Norte / Serra Talhada** - Remote, pristine campos rupestres. Mostly outside PARNA. Difficult access.
3. **Private properties along MG-010** (Km 92-100) - Cerrado and transition zones. Ask pousada owners for permission.
4. **Areas around Santana do Riacho** - Lower elevation cerrado/campos.

### Inside PARNA (SISBIO Authorization Required)

1. **Travessao / Alto Palacio** - Premier campos rupestres mapping site
2. **Canyon das Bandeirinhas** - Quartzite canyon mapping
3. **Cachoeira da Farofa area** - Cerrado-campos transition
4. **Capao area** - Forest-grassland mosaic
